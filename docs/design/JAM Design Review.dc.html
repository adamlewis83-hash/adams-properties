05 · Compliance & next steps

Oregon lease compliance

Build a compliance checklist into the lease builder — rent-increase caps and 90-day notice under SB 608, required disclosures (lead paint, flood, recycling), and fee limits. Surface a "flag for review" state so no lease deploys unchecked.

I'm not a lawyer and this isn't legal advice — have an Oregon landlord-tenant attorney review your template before relying on it.
